Swapping, without the guesswork

A concept redesign of the core swap flow in a Solana DEX aggregator. Three changes, each aimed at a reason people abandon or fail a swap.

  1. 1 · A swap card that fits every screenMobile-first layout that never clips token buttons, shows a live quote before connecting a wallet, and states the guaranteed minimum in plain words.
  2. 2 · "Swap protection" instead of hidden jargonSlippage, priority fee and MEV protection become three presets with one-line explanations. Experts still get every knob under "Custom".
  3. 3 · Failed swaps that explain themselvesWhen a swap fails, say why (price moved past your limit), confirm nothing was lost, and offer a one-tap retry with a safer setting.

Concept for a design bounty. Not affiliated with any app. Prices are illustrative.
